# Product: Lazarus

Lazarus is a browser-based CPR training coach. The phone's accelerometer measures chest-compression rate and depth live, and a ghost coach speaks one correction at a time. It also finds the nearest mapped AED. Kiroween 2026, **Frankenstein** category.

## Who it's for
- People who have never practiced CPR and want to know what "hard and fast" feels like.
- Schools, workplaces and community groups running low-cost CPR practice without feedback manikins.

## Pillars
1. **Safety first in every screen.** "Call emergency services" and "training aid, not a medical device" are always visible. Never claim Lazarus can diagnose or treat.
2. **One instruction at a time.** The coach fixes the biggest problem first and speaks briefly.
3. **Honest numbers.** If there's no steady rhythm, say so instead of showing a made-up depth.
4. **Works on the phone you have.** No install, no account, no sensor hardware beyond the phone.

## Guidelines we follow
- AHA adult CPR targets: 100–120 compressions per minute, 5–6 cm deep, full recoil, minimal pauses.
- Hands-only CPR for untrained bystanders: call, then push hard and fast in the center of the chest.

## Tone and look
- A patient monitor at midnight: black, phosphor green, alarm red for emergency actions, amber for corrections.
- The ghost is friendly and calm, a little spooky in name only. Never make light of cardiac arrest.

## Non-goals
- Use on real patients as a measuring device.
- Rescue breaths, pediatric or infant CPR in v1.
